# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Gamers and communities in regions where Discord is restricted or banned. They need a reliable, uncensored, and familiar communication platform to coordinate, chat, and build communities.

## Product Purpose
"Disco" is a desktop-style chat application offering voice, video, and text channels. It serves as a direct alternative to Discord, providing a safe haven for displaced communities to reconnect without corporate or regional restrictions.

## Brand Personality
Gamer-focused, resilient, modern, and dark. The tone is community-driven and slightly rebellious, emphasizing freedom of communication.

## Anti-references
- Corporate communication tools (like Microsoft Teams or standard Slack).
- Light, airy, or overly minimalist web chats.
- Generic social media layouts.

## Design Principles
- **Familiarity over novelty:** The transition from Discord should be frictionless. Layouts and interactions should match gamer muscle memory.
- **High-density interface:** Information (servers, channels, user statuses) should be tightly packed but easily readable, minimizing the need for scrolling.
- **Performance is a feature:** The UI must feel snappy, lightweight, and responsive, mimicking a native desktop client experience even if running in a browser environment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast requirements for dark mode legibility.
- Clear state indicators (online, offline, typing, speaking) that don't rely solely on color.
